Automating a messy process doesn't fix it.
It just makes the mess happen faster, at 3am, with nobody watching.

Before looking at any AI tool, I'd suggest a cheaper first step: a sheet of paper.

Pick one task you repeat every week. Booking confirmations, quote follow-ups, invoice reminders.

Then write down exactly what you do:

1. What starts it? An email, a form, a call.
2. What do you check before acting?
3. What do you send, and to whom?
4. What can go wrong, and what happens then?
5. When is it finished?

If it doesn't fit in five or six lines, it isn't ready to automate. Tidy it first.

If it does, you've just written the brief for the tool. Quite often the answer turns out to be a template and a calendar reminder, not AI at all.

For any step that touches customer data, two more questions: where does that data end up, and who else can see it?

Automation should take the dull part off your plate. The judgement stays with you.
