Most small business websites put their most important button in the hardest spot to tap.
The top corner of a phone screen. Exactly where your thumb doesn't reach.

Steven Hoober watched over 1,300 people using their phones in public. About half were holding it in one hand and tapping with their thumb.

For that visitor, the screen has three zones.

Bottom and middle: easy.
The edges: a stretch.
Top corners: a two-handed job.

So when I plan a page, the main action goes where the thumb already is:

1. A "Call" or "Book" button in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen.
2. Buttons sized for a thumb, not a mouse. Apple and Google both recommend touch targets of at least 44 to 48 points.
3. Nothing important hidden behind a tiny menu icon in the corner.

Try this today. Open your own site on your phone, hold it in one hand, and try to contact yourself without using the other.

If you have to shift your grip, so do your customers. Some of them won't bother.

Where does the contact button sit on your mobile site?
