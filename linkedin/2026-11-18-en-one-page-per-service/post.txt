Most small business websites have one page called "Services".
Their customers search for "boiler repair Leeds" or "emergency plumber Leeds". Google has to guess where to send them.

When one page tries to cover everything, it rarely ranks for anything in particular.

What works better for local businesses:

1. Pick the 3 to 5 services that bring in most of your work.
2. Give each one its own page, with the service and the town in the page title and the main heading.
3. On each page, answer what people ask before they call: what's included, rough timings, the areas you cover.
4. Keep one clear way to get in touch, the same on every page.
5. Link to each of them from your homepage.

This isn't keyword stuffing. It's matching the way people actually search, one need at a time.

It helps visitors too. Someone with a leaking pipe doesn't want to scroll past your bathroom gallery to find out if you do emergencies.

(The plumber in Leeds is just an example.)
